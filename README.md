# OpenArm / NexArm

A 5–6 degree-of-freedom robotic arm developed as part of the **Manufacturing for Innovation** course (MEP1001) at IIT Delhi. The project was awarded **Gold at ManufexLearn 2025** in the Research and Mega Project category.

## Overview

OpenArm (NexArm) is a servo-actuated robotic arm built to demonstrate core principles of manufacturing, mechanical design, and embedded control within an academic team project. The arm features 5–6 rotational joints, giving it a workspace and dexterity comparable to small-scale pick-and-place or educational robotic arms.

## Features

- 6 degree-of-freedom articulated arm
- Servo motor actuation at each joint
- Microcontroller-based control (Arduino)
- Designed and manufactured as part of a collaborative team project

## Team & Role

Developed by the OpenArm/NexArm team as part of MEP1001. Contributed across multiple areas of the project — including design, manufacturing, and assembly — as part of a general team role.
